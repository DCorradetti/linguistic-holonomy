# Manuscript

`Article_LLW_v2.tex` is the current results-complete draft of:

> *Linguistic Holonomy and the Erosion of Statistical Watermarks: Meaning-Preserving Transformations as Paths, not Endpoints.*

The rendered file is available at [`output/pdf/Linguistic_Holonomy_draft_v2.pdf`](../output/pdf/Linguistic_Holonomy_draft_v2.pdf).

The v2 source incorporates the expanded study of 540 real rewriting chains, the preregistered verdict, clustered and passage-fixed robustness checks, the matched-endpoint analysis, and the regeneration audit. It is still not a final submission; the remaining empirical and review gates are listed in [`STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md).

The original [`Article_LLW_v1.tex`](Article_LLW_v1.tex) and its rendered PDF are preserved unchanged so that the public history remains auditable.
